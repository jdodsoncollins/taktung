Check your Vercel deployments from your iPhone or iPad. Taktung brings production status, build logs, visual comparison and a local Field Log into one place.

- See your site's production state and recent deployments.
- Compare real page captures with a reveal slider or side-by-side view. Save a baseline for the next deployment.
- Investigate failed builds and read their logs. Check environment variable names and targets without exposing values.
- Keep checks, comparisons and personal notes in a searchable local Field Log with text export.
- Add small, medium or large Home Screen widgets. Choose all projects or one project; the large widget also shows recent deployment activity.
- Ask Siri for deployment status, or use Shortcuts to inspect a project and check its loaded signals.
- Review and confirm redeploy, promote and rollback actions.

Connect with a Vercel personal access token, stored in the device Keychain. Taktung calls Vercel directly; history and saved baselines stay on this device, separated by account and team.

Choose Light, Dark or System appearance. Layouts adapt to iPhone, expanded iPhone Duo and iPad.

On compatible devices with Apple Intelligence available, site briefs can be summarized locally. Standard diagnosis remains available on every supported device.

Requires iOS 27 or later and a Vercel account.
